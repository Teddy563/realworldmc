# RealWorldMC 1.0.1: Earth to orbit

Fly from real places into Earth orbit, then take a look around the Moon and Mars. This update adds experimental space flight to **Minecraft Java 1.21.1 with Fabric**.

### New in 1.0.1

- **Fly into space.** In a Creative, singleplayer Real World world, climb above the default 2 km entry height or use `/earth space`. Hold Space to rise, Shift to descend and WASD to travel. No elytra is needed, and speed increases as you climb.
- **Come back down.** Descending below the default 1.6 km exit height loads the place beneath you and resumes normal Creative flight. `/earth space return` returns to your original launch point.
- **Visit the Moon and Mars.** Use `/earth moon`, `/earth mars` and `/earth earth`, or hold forward while aiming at a destination marker above 100 km. The planetary images are bundled and need no extra imagery API key.
- **Record steadier shots.** Globe motion is smoother, loaded scenery stays visible during tile changes and the third-person player stays visible and lit. Flight poses follow your movement without changing your equipment.
- **Keep Earth objects out of orbit.** Local blocks, mobs and particles are hidden while orbiting. You cannot accidentally target hidden Earth objects, and your launch world is preserved.
- **Read controls more easily.** Short hints now sit above the bottom-left credits. `/earth actionbar` toggles them. Street View arrows and browser navigation buttons are larger.
- **Handle loading interruptions better.** OSM water generation has a setup toggle, a request timeout and a cooldown after failure. Bundled services use fallback ports if their usual ports are occupied.

### Update and try it

Back up your worlds and close Minecraft. Replace the old RealWorldMC JAR with `RealWorldMC-1.0.1-mc1.21.1.jar`, keeping only one copy installed. Existing keys and settings stay in place. Use Fabric, Fabric API and Java 21.

Start with `/earth space` in a Creative, singleplayer Real World world. Use the normal camera toggle for third person. Try `/earth moon` or `/earth mars`, then `/earth space return` to go home.

### Limits to know

- Space flight is experimental. Earth descent can pause for map loading and still depends on provider coverage, credentials and usage limits.
- Moon and Mars are **orbital image flyovers**. You cannot land, walk, mine or build on their surfaces yet.
- The 26.2 module remains experimental and does **not** include native space flight or in-game Street View.
- Bliss produced a blank Earth return view in an offline test with both the previous and current JAR. This remains unresolved; shader compatibility is not guaranteed.
- Optional camera, cape, aircraft and superhero mods from the recording profile are separate add-ons, not bundled RealWorldMC features.

See [realworldmc.com](https://realworldmc.com/) for installation, [the space guide](https://github.com/Teddy563/realworldmc/blob/main/docs/space-flight.md) for controls and [accounts and costs](https://github.com/Teddy563/realworldmc/blob/main/docs/accounts-and-costs.md) before using map APIs.
